How WinterArc uses your health data

If you connect Health Connect, WinterArc reads three things from it, and nothing else:

  • Daily step totals, to show your steps and progress toward your goal.
  • Weight, to chart your trend over time.
  • Resting heart rate, to chart it over time.

What happens to it

These daily values are sent over an encrypted connection to your WinterArc account and stored with the rest of your data. They are used only to show you your own progress. They are not shared with anyone, not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. The AI assistant only sees them if you ask it about your fitness, and only for your own account.

WinterArc never writes to Health Connect.

Your control

You can stop syncing at any time in the Fitness page, and revoke access in Health Connect settings. You can export all your data or delete your account (which erases everything, including synced health values) from Settings. Deleting a value in the app removes it from your account.

The full Privacy Policy is available in the app at Settings → Privacy and on our website at /privacy.
